Let’s Turn Your Production Into a Release-Ready Record

Andre Kelman is a New York City-based audio engineer, music producer, mixer, sound designer with over 20 years of professional experience in music, film, TV, animation and advertising.

What I Do Contact

What I Do

I provide high-quality audio services across every stage of production — from recording, editing, and mixing to sound design, Foley, and music composition. Whether it’s a feature film, TV series, commercial, or album, I focus on creating sound that enhances the story, emotion, and impact of every project.

Next

Who I Am

I’m a creative problem solver who believes great sound is equal parts art and precision. Collaboration drives my process — whether I’m working with directors, producers, or musicians, my goal is always the same: to deliver work that sounds incredible, feels authentic, and serves the vision at hand.
